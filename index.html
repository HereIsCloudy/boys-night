Instructions

Select a tool, hover over the floor to see the placement preview, then click to place or erase. Use orbit controls to move the camera around the scene.

Walls and doors are shown as vertical structures. Shelves sit on the floor and walkways are translucent floor tiles.

Use Auto layout to create a game-style interior layout with door spacing, shelves, and walkways that fit the grid size.

This page runs entirely in the browser and is designed for clean 3D base layout design.
